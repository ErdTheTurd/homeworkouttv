Home Workout TV

For Apple TV

Follow-along workouts, right on your TV.

Home Workout TV is a simple way to move at home. Choose a built-in bodyweight session, then follow each move with a timer and short form cues — no equipment and no account required.

How it works

  1. Pick a session

    Browse a short list of built-in workouts on your TV, each with a warm-up, timed moves, and a cooldown.

  2. Follow along

    The player shows the current move, a countdown, a plain form cue, and what’s next — large enough to read from the couch.

  3. Optional heart rate, planned

    Live heart rate from Apple Watch is planned as an optional extra. It is not available yet.

A note on safety

Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.

Move at a pace that feels right for you, and skip anything that doesn’t. Home Workout TV is a follow-along exercise app, not medical advice.
