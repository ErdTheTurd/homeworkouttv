For Apple TV

Four sessions.
On the TV.

Gentle Mobility, Easy Full Body, Easy Core, and Seated Stretch. Each one is about five minutes: a warm-up, timed moves, and a cooldown. The current move, a countdown, and a form cue stay on screen.

The list on the TV

Pick one of four built-in bodyweight sessions. No equipment. No account.

What’s on screen

Get set
A short ready count shows the first move and its form cue, so you can get in place before the session starts.
Current move
Large type, plus a short form cue — for example, “Sit hips back. Keep heels down.”
Countdown
A timer for that move, big enough to read from the couch. Skip a move, or leave, if it does not feel right.
Next
The upcoming move, or Finish, with a row of ticks for how far you are through the session.
Heart rate Not in this version
Live heart rate from Apple Watch is planned, not shipping yet. Today the panel reads “No watch connected.”

Safety

Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.

Move at a pace that feels right for you, and skip anything that doesn’t. Home Workout TV is a follow-along exercise app, not medical advice.
